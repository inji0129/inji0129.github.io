About

I enjoy simplifying repetitive work and improving the systems behind running services. When building a feature, I consider how people will use it and what it will take to operate it day to day.

Seeing operations staff repeatedly rely on developers or connect to servers to complete routine tasks led me to build internal web tools they could use themselves. Changing how work gets done through software was rewarding, and it made me look for ways to remove manual work whenever I encountered it.

Expanding services across countries and migrating existing APIs taught me to look beyond the code being changed to the features and operations that depend on it. I am particularly interested in making meaningful structural changes while keeping existing services running.

With re:feel, an app I co-planned with a friend, I also built and operated a product myself. I developed the app and backend, handled deployment, and used usage data and feedback to refine its target audience and features after launch. I enjoy seeing how people use a product and turning their responses into the next improvement.

I now use MCP to bring product specifications and related code into context, and work with AI on implementation plans to speed up development. I review generated code myself and base architecture and technology decisions on the needs of the service and its operating environment.
